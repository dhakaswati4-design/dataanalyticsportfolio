## Internship Dataset

The cleaned sales dataset contains **1,000 records** and 12 core fields
including order, customer, date, location, product, category, quantity,
price and total sales information.

## Task 4 Statistical Validation

The tested hypothesis asked whether average order-level **Total Sales**
differs between Male and Female customers.

-   Test: Welch's independent two-sample t-test
-   Significance level: 0.05
-   Male mean Total Sales: approximately ₹141,807
-   Female mean Total Sales: approximately ₹136,883
-   t-statistic: approximately 0.683
-   p-value: approximately 0.495
-   Decision: Fail to reject H0

The sample did not provide sufficient statistical evidence of a
difference in mean order-level Total Sales between the two groups at the
5% significance level.
