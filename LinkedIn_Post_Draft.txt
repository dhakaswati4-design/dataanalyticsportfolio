I’m happy to share the completion of my ApexPlanet 60-Days Data
Analytics Internship.

During the internship, I worked through an end-to-end analytics
workflow:

• Data cleaning and preparation • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) •
SQL-based business analysis • KPI and dashboard development • Data
storytelling • Statistical hypothesis testing • GitHub portfolio
development

For the final statistical validation, I tested whether average
order-level Total Sales differed between Male and Female customers using
Welch’s independent two-sample t-test. The p-value was approximately
0.495 at a 0.05 significance level, so the sample did not provide
sufficient statistical evidence of a difference in mean order-level
Total Sales between the two groups.
