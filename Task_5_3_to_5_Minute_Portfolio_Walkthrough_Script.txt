APEXPLANET TASK 5 – 3–5 MINUTE PORTFOLIO WALKTHROUGH

Hello everyone. This is my final portfolio walkthrough for the
ApexPlanet 60-Days Data Analytics Internship.

This master portfolio brings together the work I completed across Tasks
1 to 4 and presents the overall analytics journey.

In Task 1, I worked on data immersion and wrangling. I profiled the
sales dataset, checked data quality issues such as missing values and
duplicates, performed cleaning and transformation, and prepared an
analysis-ready dataset.

In Task 2, I moved to exploratory data analysis and business
intelligence. I used descriptive statistics, business questions,
SQL-based analysis and visualizations to understand patterns and
relationships in the data.

In Task 3, I focused on deep-dive analysis and dashboarding. I defined
key performance indicators and built an interactive dashboard to help
explore sales patterns across areas such as time, category, city,
product and customer-related dimensions.

In Task 4, I converted the earlier analysis into a business story and
added statistical validation. One hypothesis tested whether average
order-level Total Sales differed between Male and Female customers. I
used Welch’s independent two-sample t-test with a 0.05 significance
level. The p-value was approximately 0.495, so the sample did not
provide sufficient statistical evidence of a difference in mean
order-level Total Sales between the two groups.

Some descriptive findings from the analysis were that Electronics had
the highest category sales, Patna had the highest city total among the
listed cities, and March 2025 was the highest-sales month in the
observed data.

The main skills demonstrated across the internship include Excel, Python
and Pandas workflow, SQL, data cleaning, EDA, data visualization, KPI
analysis, dashboarding, statistical testing, GitHub and business
presentation.

The biggest learning for me was understanding how an analyst connects
the complete process: first preparing reliable data, then asking
business questions, analyzing patterns, building dashboards, validating
findings statistically and finally communicating the results clearly.

This repository is the single reference point for my internship work,
with links to the four task-specific repositories and the final
presentation.

Thank you for watching my portfolio walkthrough.
